Deutsch A1 respects your privacy. No account registration is required to study, and we do not sell personal user data.

  • Local Progress Storage: Lesson progress, vocabulary mastery, and settings are stored locally on your device via localStorage.
  • Advertising (Free Tier): The free version may display ads via third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) in accordance with Apple App Store and Google Play policies.
  • Subscriptions (Deutsch A1 Premium — 5 GEL / month): Monthly subscriptions remove all ads and daily usage limits. Payments are processed securely by Apple App Store or Google Play; we never access or store your payment card details.
  • Contact: For privacy questions or subscription support, contact the app developer via the store listing support email.
